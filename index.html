PROPOSED RESEARCH

Digital Financial Inclusion and Household Financial Resilience to Flood Shocks

Evidence from flood-prone Sindh, Pakistan

The problem

Flood shocks can disrupt household income, savings, payments and access to formal financial services. This proposed study examines whether digital financial access can help households manage these temporary financial disruptions.

Main question

To what extent does access to digital financial services improve household financial resilience following flood shocks in flood-prone Sindh?

Possible focus

  • Digital banking and mobile financial services
  • Household liquidity and savings resilience
  • Emergency payments and remittances
  • Financial literacy and inclusion
  • Differences across rural and vulnerable households

Proposed approach

Quantitative empirical analysis using financial inclusion indicators, flood exposure measures and household financial-resilience outcomes, subject to data availability and supervisor guidance.
